Land Ecosystem
- Description
The Land Ecosystem represents terrestrial environments that play a vital role in maintaining ecological balance and supporting life. Through this donation category, EarthScape invites users to contribute to the preservation and restoration of land ecosystems affected by human activities and climate change.
- Overview
The Land Ecosystem donation program focuses on protecting and sustainably managing terrestrial environments. Collected donations support conservation efforts aimed at maintaining soil quality, biodiversity, and the long-term sustainability of land-based ecosystems.
- Impact
Contributions to this program help reduce land degradation and improve overall ecosystem health. In the long term, these efforts support more stable, resilient, and sustainable land ecosystems that benefit both nature and surrounding communities.

Forestation
- Description
Forestation focuses on restoring green areas through tree planting and forest rehabilitation. This initiative supports efforts to rebuild forest ecosystems that have been reduced due to deforestation and land-use changes.
- Overview
The Forestation donation program is dedicated to supporting tree planting and forest recovery initiatives. Donations are used to help restore forest cover, improve biodiversity, and strengthen ecosystems that play an essential role in regulating the climate.
- Impact
By contributing to this program, users help increase forest resilience and reduce environmental damage. These efforts support cleaner air, healthier ecosystems, and a more sustainable future for both wildlife and local communities.

Marine Ecosystem
- Description
Marine Ecosystem focuses on protecting and restoring ocean and coastal environments. This initiative supports conservation efforts aimed at preserving marine biodiversity and maintaining healthy underwater ecosystems.
- Overview
The Marine Ecosystem donation program is designed to support activities such as coral reef restoration, coastal protection, and marine habitat conservation. Donations contribute to initiatives that help maintain ocean balance and protect marine life from environmental threats.
- Impact
Through this program, users help safeguard marine ecosystems that are vital for biodiversity and coastal communities. The impact includes healthier oceans, improved marine habitats, and long-term sustainability for future generations.
